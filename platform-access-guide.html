Before you start

Your business stays in control. You remain the owner of your accounts and data. Noord receives access only to manage the agreed services.

No passwords to share. Use an account with administrator permissions to grant access. You do not need to send us your personal password.

01 / Meta

Facebook, Instagram & Meta Ads

We use Meta Business Suite to manage Facebook and Instagram advertising, campaigns, leads and performance.

  1. Go to Meta Business Settings, log in, and select the correct Business Portfolio.
  2. In the left menu, select Users → Partners. Click Add, then Give a partner access to your assets.
  3. Enter Noord's Business Portfolio ID: 757445217315771, then click Next.
  4. Select the assets Noord needs: your Facebook Page, Instagram account, advertising account, Dataset / Meta Pixel, and any other relevant advertising assets.
  5. Grant the permissions needed for Noord to manage the agreed advertising campaigns, leads and assets. Click Assign assets.
  6. Confirm Noord Growth appears under your Business Portfolio's Partners section, then let us know.
02 / Search

Google Search Console

Search Console helps us monitor your website's performance in Google Search and find opportunities to improve visibility.

  1. Open Google Search Console and sign in with the Google account that manages your website.
  2. Select the correct website property in the top-left corner.
  3. Open Settings → Users and permissions → Add user.
  4. Enter go@noordgrowth.co.za, choose Full permission, and click Add.
  5. Confirm Noord's email appears in the users list.

You must be a verified property owner to add users. If Search Console has not been set up, Noord can help with setup and verification.

03 / Advertising

Google Ads

Noord connects to your existing Google Ads account through a Manager account. Your business keeps ownership and billing control.

  1. Sign in at Google Ads and select the correct advertising account.
  2. Find and copy your 10-digit Customer ID, formatted like 123-456-7890.
  3. Send the Customer ID to go@noordgrowth.co.za. Noord will send a manager-account linking request.
  4. Once we confirm it has been sent, open Admin → Access and security → Managers in Google Ads.
  5. Find Noord's pending linking request, click Accept, and confirm. We will check the connection.

No Google Ads account yet? Noord can help create and configure one.

04 / Analytics

Google Analytics

Analytics helps us understand visitor behaviour, conversion tracking and which marketing activities generate results.

  1. Open Google Analytics, sign in and select the correct GA4 property.
  2. Click Admin, then under Property select Property access management.
  3. Click +, choose Add users, and enter go@noordgrowth.co.za.
  4. Choose the Editor role, keep email notification enabled if available, and click Add.
  5. Confirm Noord appears in the users list.

Editor lets Noord configure analytics, events and conversion tracking. Administrator access, if needed for user management, will be requested separately. If Analytics is not installed yet, Noord can help.

05 / Website

WordPress

For standard self-hosted WordPress sites, a separate Noord user lets us make agreed content, design, SEO and technical improvements.

  1. Open your site's administration page, usually yourwebsite.co.za/wp-admin, and log in with your Administrator account.
  2. Go to Users → Add New User.
  3. Set username noordgrowth, email go@noordgrowth.co.za, first name Noord, and last name Growth Systems.
  4. Select Administrator if Noord will manage design, plugins, technical SEO and website settings. For content-only work, Editor may be sufficient.
  5. Enable the email notification if available, click Add New User, and confirm Noord appears under Users → All Users.
  6. Let us know the account is ready. Noord will use the invitation or password setup process to access it securely.

WordPress access does not include hosting, domain registrar or DNS access. We will discuss those separately if needed.

Final step

Tell us what's connected.

Only complete the platforms relevant to your services. Then let Noord know which of these are ready:

  • Meta Business Suite — Noord added as a business partner
  • Google Search Console — Full user access granted
  • Google Ads — Customer ID shared and manager request accepted
  • Google Analytics — Editor access granted
  • WordPress — Noord user created

Send us a WhatsApp confirmation or email go@noordgrowth.co.za. We'll check the permissions and let you know if anything else is needed.
