Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

Your Privacy Matters

At Noord Growth Systems ("Noord", "we", "our", or "us"), we believe trust is built through transparency.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we use it.

By using our website or engaging with our services, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with Noord, we may collect:

Information you provide

When you book a Growth Session, complete a contact form, download a resource, subscribe to updates, or become a client, we may collect information including:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Company name
  • Business information
  • Any information you voluntarily provide.

Information collected automatically

When visiting our website we may automatically collect information such as:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device information
  • Pages visited
  • Time spent on pages
  • Referral source

This information helps us improve our website and services.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to enquiries
  • Schedule Growth Sessions
  • Deliver our consulting services
  • Improve our website
  • Improve our Growth Systems
  • Communicate important updates
  • Send marketing communications where you've opted in
  • Comply with legal obligations

Marketing Communications

If you choose to receive updates from Noord, we may occasionally send business insights, growth resources, company updates, and new services.

You can unsubscribe at any time using the link included in our emails.

Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to understand website performance, improve user experience, measure marketing effectiveness, and remember preferences.

You can disable cookies through your browser settings.

Third-Party Services

To operate our business we may use trusted third-party providers including:

  • Google Workspace
  • Google Analytics
  • Google Calendar
  • Calendly
  • CRM platforms
  • Email marketing platforms
  • Payment providers
  • Website hosting providers

These providers only receive the information necessary to perform their services and are responsible for handling data according to their own privacy policies.

How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information from unauthorised access, loss, misuse, disclosure, and alteration.

While no online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, protecting your information is important to us.

Data Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as necessary to deliver our services, maintain business records, meet legal or accounting obligations, and resolve disputes.

After this period, information is securely deleted or anonymised where appropriate.

Your Rights

Depending on applicable privacy laws, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion
  • Withdraw marketing consent
  • Request a copy of your information

To exercise these rights, please contact us.

Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for business owners and organisations. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those websites.

Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

The latest version will always be available on this page, together with the revision date.
